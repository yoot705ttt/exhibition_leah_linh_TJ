Universe 03 / Color, shape, contrast

Leah’s universe

The inspiration

After Peter Halley

Artist
Peter Halley

Why I chose it

I chose this Peter Halley artwork because I really like its bold colors, simple geometric shapes, and strong contrast. The composition is mainly made of rectangles and straight lines, which makes it look simple but still visually interesting. I thought this artwork would be a good choice to recreate with HTML and CSS because I could use basic shapes, borders, and positioning to build the composition. I was also interested in seeing how a physical painting could be translated into a digital format using code.

— Leah
